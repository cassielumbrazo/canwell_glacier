CANWELL HRRR-AK NETCDF: SNOWPACK FORCING GUIDE

Companion file: hrrrak_canwell_f567_WY2020-WY2026.nc

Use the grid cell nearest the site for the Canwell workflow. The
two-dimensional latitude and longitude arrays give the cell centers.
Extract one value per hour from that cell. Use time as the timestamp:
it is the forecast valid time in UTC. Set the SMET time zone to 0 (UTC).

NETCDF VARIABLE(S)    SMET FIELD   WHAT TO WRITE
-------------------   ----------   -------------------------------------------
temp                  TA           2 m air temperature in kelvin (K).
rh                    RH           2 m relative humidity as a fraction.
                                   Divide the NetCDF percent value by 100.
wind                  VW           10 m wind speed in m/s. Already computed
                                   from 10u and 10v.
10u, 10v              DW           10 m wind direction in degrees clockwise
                                   from true north, measured from the
                                   direction the wind comes from. The two
                                   components follow the HRRR grid axes.
                                   Account for grid rotation using the crs
                                   metadata, then wrap the result to [0, 360).
swrad                 ISWR         Incoming shortwave radiation in W/m^2.
                                   Set negative values to zero.
lwrad                 ILWR         Incoming longwave radiation in W/m^2.
precip_hourly         PSUM         Total precipitation during the one-hour
                                   interval ending at the timestamp. The
                                   NetCDF unit kg/m^2 is numerically equal
                                   to mm of water equivalent. Copy it directly.
pressure              P            Surface air pressure in pascals (Pa).

SMET column order:
timestamp TA RH VW DW ISWR ILWR PSUM P

NOTES

- Use precip_hourly for hourly PSUM. precip_rate is a rate, precip_total
  accumulates from the forecast initialization, and snowfall_total is
  snowfall depth. SNOWPACK partitions PSUM into rain and snow in the model
  configuration. The Look Eyrie runs used a temperature threshold of 1.2 C.
- orog is the grid-cell surface geopotential height in meters. Use it with
  the site elevation if applying a lapse-rate correction to TA. Keep the
  site elevation in the SMET header.
- Check that the extracted timestamps are hourly and the required fields
  are present. A missing precipitation value should be treated as missing.
